## Description
Draw letters with your finger. stupidfont turns them into a real font, live, while you draw.

Every letter can have up to five takes. The font cycles through them as you type, so a word like MOON never shows the same O twice. That's what makes a hand-drawn font feel hand-drawn instead of stamped.

You start with Pip3 Beef, a wobbly felt-tip alphabet drawn by the artist 60R90, with all its alternates already loaded. Redraw any letter, any take, or clear the lot and start from your own hand.

What you get
- A real OpenType font (.otf) that works in any app that takes fonts: design tools, word processors, video editors, the lot.
- Up to five takes per letter, cycled automatically with standard font features. No plugin needed on the other end.
- Guides for baseline, x-height and cap height so your letters line up.
- Pen, eraser, brush size, undo, and a ghost of your first take while you draw the others.
- Load font: pull any .ttf or .otf in and edit it letter by letter. Alternates become takes.
- Line: rough, normal or neat, for felt-tip texture or clean vector edges.
- Save and load projects. Share the finished font straight to Files, AirDrop or any app.

No account. No sign-in. Nothing leaves your phone. Your drawings autosave on the device.
