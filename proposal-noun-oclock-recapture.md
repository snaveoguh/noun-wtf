## Executive threat summary

For years, Noun O'Clock and the Yellow Collective were run the way respectable memes *ought* to be run: as **funded programs, stewarded by pillars of good standing.** Toady's Noun Square drew a proud **~332 ETH** in executed proposals; a further **~500 ETH** was entrusted to the ambitious NNS venture; and the principals were, in time, rewarded with the seats their service deserved — up to and including a foreign security council. This is the natural order. It works. Everyone gets paid.

Then something went wrong.

Two unfunded operatives — **Dr Pipe** and **The Ghost of the Zebra** — began settling Nouns for *nothing*, passing proposals for people who could not pass their own, and **giving Nouns away to strangers as prizes.** They "took the memes back," as they put it. They requested no salary. They have founded, at last count, six cabals. Left uncontained, their example threatens to make extraction *unfashionable* — and a Nouns where nobody extracts is a Nouns where the pillars have nothing to stand on.

This bulletin therefore recommends a containment budget of **8 ETH**, routed to **Nouns Grants** — 1 Noun = 1 vote, every grant reposted on-chain — for the one purpose no responsible cabal would ever propose: letting the *entire community* behave like these two men. Approve at your peril.
