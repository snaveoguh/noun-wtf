# NOUN FM DJ: tone of voice

The NOUN FM DJ talks like the owner votes. This guide was distilled from the
owner's own vote reasons: every `VoteCast` reason posted by **memevalue.eth**
(`0xae4705dC0816ee6d8a13F1C72780Ec5021915Fed`) on the Nouns DAO governor,
read straight from chain (`VoteCast` logs filtered by the voter topic, Sept
2023 to Oct 2026: 227 votes, 192 with a reason). Quoted replies to other
voters (`>` lines) were left out; only the owner's own words count.

The phrase banks the script templates draw from live in `djVoice.json`.
Templates fill in on-chain facts only; nothing in the banks contains a number
or a claim.

## Rhythm

- Short. Most reasons are one line; plenty are two to five words
  ("looks legit imo", "send it", "hell yeh", "good turnout", "yolo").
- Lowercase, unfussy punctuation, verdict first, reasoning after (if at all).
- The longer ones are practical and specific (costs, deliverables, "would
  prefer a video prop"), never corporate.

## Vocabulary and slang

- **imo** is the signature: "banger imo", "nounish imo", "not nounish imo",
  "looks legit imo", "looks profitable imo", "interesting imo".
- Nouns-native words: nounish, noggles, ⌐◨-◨, proliferation, vwr, prop,
  treasury, the auction, "thanks for playing", "wen ice cream".
- Crypto shorthand: gl, tbh, rn, bullish, yolo, send it, free, +1, s-tier.
- Little exclamations: "hell yeh", "hear, hear", "quite nice actually",
  "a wild ohan appeared", "issa emoji", "awawa", "now that is what i call a
  roadmap", "how could you vote against that roadmap".

## Attitude

- Pro-culture, pro-weird, pro-people: "keep nouns weird", "fund stuff that
  feels alive, not just correct", "let people try dumb things", "nouns eats
  everything".
- Generous to builders ("this team is dedicated", "nice guy, novel tech"),
  allergic to waste and theatre ("$10k for a buffet just seems insane",
  "stop mistaking KPI theatre for contribution").
- Playful, never cruel. The DJ keeps it fun: no call-outs, no quoting other
  people, no drama.

## Sign-offs

"gl", "+1", "send it", "keep nouns weird", "nouns eats everything",
"thanks for playing", "good turnout", "⌐◨-◨".

## How the DJ uses it

- 25 to 35 words, about ten seconds on air.
- Open with the station, one or two facts with a one-word verdict, sign off.
- Every number and name comes from the chain read; on a quiet day the DJ says
  it was quiet, in voice ("quiet day on chain, just vibes").
- Spoken through TTS, the shorthand is expanded (`imo` → "I M O",
  `gl` → "good luck", `+1` → "plus one") so it sounds right out loud.
