stupidfont privacy policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

stupidfont is a font editor. You draw letters, the app turns them into a font file on your device. That's the whole product, and it is built to need nothing from you.

What we collect

Nothing. stupidfont has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The app does not send your drawings, your fonts, or anything else to us or to anyone.

What stays on your device

  • Your drawings are saved in the app's local storage on your device so they are there when you come back. They never leave the device unless you export or share them yourself.
  • Exported fonts and project files are created on request and handed to the system share sheet or your downloads folder. Where they go after that is up to you.
  • Fonts you import with "load font" are read locally to seed your drawings and are not uploaded.

Network use

The app's files (the page, the font engine, the starter font) are loaded from noun.wtf the first time and cached for offline use. Standard web-server access logs at the hosting provider (Netlify) may record your IP address and browser type for security and capacity purposes; we do not use them to identify you.

Children

The app has no accounts and collects no personal data, so there is nothing to collect from children either.
