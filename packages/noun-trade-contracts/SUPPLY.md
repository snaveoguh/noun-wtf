# Noun Trade supply model

How NTCOIN and the goods tokens come into existence on Base, how fast, and how they
leave again. Every number here is enforced by `src/NounTradeGoods.sol` with the values in
`script/TradeConfig.sol`. The game server mirrors them (`CLAIM_CAPS` in
`packages/nouns-webapp/src/miniapps/world2/trade/onchain/tokens.ts`) so it refuses a
cash-out before the chain would.

## The short version

- **No premine. Nothing exists until a player earns it.** Tokens are only minted when a
  player cashes out balance they earned in the game: driving goods between towns,
  selling them, and serving customers. Each cash-out needs a server-signed EIP-712
  voucher that the player submits from their own wallet.
- **The 150-coin starter purse never leaves the game.** You can spend it in the game,
  but you can only cash out coin above it (`claimableCoin` in the ledger).
- **Ice cream melts.** ICECREAM can't be cashed out. Its on-chain cap and max supply are
  both 0, so it is never a token.
- **Emission decays.** Each token's global daily cap halves every 180 days after launch.
  It halves four times, then stays at 1/16 as a small tail.
- **Hard ceilings.** Each token has a lifetime mint ceiling (`maxSupply`). The admin can
  lower it but never raise it. Burns don't give the headroom back.
- **Caps only go down.** `setCap` can only lower a cap. The only way to add supply is to
  launch a brand-new good id, once, with `launchGood`.
- **Sinks.** Depositing tokens back into the game burns them. In the game, coin is spent
  on goods and van upgrades, and ice cream melts in the van.

## Launch parameters

| key | token | per wallet / day | global / day (launch) | after 180 d | after 720 d (floor) | lifetime max supply |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | NTCOIN (ERC-20, 0 decimals) | 5,000 | 500,000 | 250,000 | 31,250 | 210,000,000 |
| 1 | ICECREAM | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 (melts) |
| 2–7 | FRIES, FISH, DRIP, NOGGLES, PIZZA, WATER (ERC-1155), each | 200 | 20,000 | 10,000 | 1,250 | 20,000,000 |

The per-wallet cap is about 8 days of a regular player's earnings. It's there so one
whale can't fill the day's global cap on their own. Days are UTC
(`block.timestamp / 1 days`). The halving counts from `launchDay`, the UTC day of the
deploy, which `deployments/8453.json` records.

If demand fills every global cap, NTCOIN hits its 210M ceiling about 5.6 years after
launch. The first two years mint at most 168.75M; after that the tail emission
(31,250/day) takes about 3.6 more years to mint the remaining ~41M. Real demand is
lower: in the steady scenario below, the first year mints about 112M.

## Flows

```
            drive · buy · sell · serve        cash out (voucher, capped)
 game COIN  ─────────────────────────▶ ledger ──────────────────────────▶ NTCOIN / goods on Base
 (off-chain)◀───────────────────────── books  ◀────────────────────────── deposit (burn)
            spend: goods, upgrades             credited once per tx
            sink: ice cream melts              sink: burned supply never comes back
```

- **Mint:** `claim(voucher, sig)`. The server first debits the rider's game books, then
  signs. The voucher is bound to the player's address, has a nonce and an expiry, and
  counts against the caps.
- **Burn:** `deposit(ids, amounts, coin, keccak256(riderId))`. The server reads the
  receipt and credits the rider's books once per tx.
- **Refund:** if a voucher expires unused, it is refunded to the game books.
- **Books:** `coin = granted + earned + in − spent − out`, audited on every write
  (`audit` in `trade/ledger.ts`).

- clears about 600 COIN of profit a day;

**Reading it.**
- **Small game (100 DAU).** Supply settles around 2.7M NTCOIN. Deposits burn about as
  much as players cash out, and no cap ever binds.
- **Steady growth (1,000 DAU, +10%/mo).** The global cap starts to clip at the first
  halving (month 7). From then on, burns outpace mints and circulating supply drifts
  down even as players double.
- **Breakout (10,000 DAU, +25%/mo).** The caps bind from day one, so NTCOIN issuance is
  close to steady growth's: the cap is what holds it, however many players there are.
  Goods hit their global caps from month 3.

**What would change it.**
- The admin can lower any cap or ceiling at any time, for example if bots farm the
  game. It can never raise them.
- The server's anti-cheat (travel time between towns, rate limits, per-customer
  cooldowns in `trade/ledger.ts`) bounds what one rider can earn, before any of the
  chain caps apply.
